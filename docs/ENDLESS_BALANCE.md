evolutions, combos, monster roster, chests, events, pearls) and the real reel physics (ReelSim). Client-only formulas (fish tier
roll, fish coins, rig behaviours, kill rewards, spill, hazards, monster and boss AI timings) are mirrored in one function each,
marked `// mirrors <file> <method>`: when you tune those in `client/src/endless`, change the mirror too. Dodging, loot pickup and
the player's harpoon aim are probability models driven by the bot constants at the top of the script (casual / average / skilled).
Treat the numbers as relative (A vs B after a change), not as exact player outcomes.

**Why each change** (file: old → new)

*Economy (income flooded: ×60 from tides 1–5 to 16–20 while prices rose 8.5%/tide)*
- `rules.ts` prices: `1 + 0.085(t−1) + 0.06·over` → `priceMultOf(t) = 1.13^(t−1) × (1 + 0.05·over)`, used by rigs, trinkets and rerolls.
- `data.ts` base prices ×2: trinkets 12/24/42/75/120 → 24/48/84/150/240; every rig `price` doubled (Bobber 16 → 32 … Ink Jet 30 → 60).
- `rules.ts` start coins 15 → 25 → 15 (the first shop buys about 2 items); Harbour Seed Money +8 → +15 coins a level.
- `rules.ts` reroll `ceil(1+0.6t) + n·ceil(1+0.35t)` → `(10 + 10n) × priceMult`; level-up reroll `2 + ⌊t/2⌋` → `4 + t`; sell value 40% → 30%; interest cap `10 + 3t` → `15 + 4t`.
- `rules.ts` fish supply: schools `3 + ⌊t/4⌋` → `3 + ⌊t/8⌋`; school size `[4+⌊t/5⌋, 7+⌊t/4⌋]` → `[4+⌊t/10⌋, 7+⌊t/8⌋]`.
- `EndlessMode.rollTier` (+ mirror): tide bonus on rare/gold/epic/legendary weights `1 + t×0.02/0.05/0.07/0.09` → `0.015/0.03/0.04/0.05`.
- `data.ts` `FISH_COINS` epic 30 → 25, legendary 80 → 60. Kill coins `1 + ⌊t/2⌋` → `2 + t` (fighters get a share now that prices are higher).
- `data.ts` Golden Hook `max: 2` (goldBonus stacked without limit).

*Stat stacking (damage × fire rate × engineering, haul × luck, armor and HP all stacked linearly)*
- `rules.ts` new `STAT_SOFT_CAPS` applied at the end of `computeStats` (so the stat panel, shop luck and runtime all see the
  same value): full value up to a knee, then hyperbolic to knee + room. harpoon 40+110, fireRate 30+60, engineering 40+90,
  haul 25+45, luck 40+90, regen 1.5+2.5, range 30+30, armor 12+18, maxHp 180+170. `STAT_INFO` texts mention it.
- `CRIT_CAP` 0.75 → 0.5 (`EndlessMode.refreshStats` + mirror).
- `EndlessMode.updateRigs` (+ mirror): Engineering no longer adds damage, only speed (harpoon rigs lost `×(1 + eng/200)`,
  charges and drones lost `×eng`; charges and drones now get `×(1 + harpoon/200)`). Tackle Box eng 7 → 5, Overclock Gear
  22 → 16, Abyss Lantern 12 → 8, level-up step 4 → 3.
- Depth Charge power 38/62/96/145 → 42/70/110/170 and Lantern Drone 6/10/16/25 → 8/14/22/34 (the non-harpoon damage rigs
  were never picked once engineering stopped carrying them).

*Evolutions (Midas in 98% of average runs; Gatling/Tsunami/Storm in ~90% of skilled runs; several copies of one ★ rig)*
- `evolutions.ts`: each evolution exists once per run (`hasEvolution`), and evolving costs coins: `evolveCost` = 1.5× the
  Tier IV price of that rig (priced as at least a 40-coin rig). The shop button shows the price.
- Midas Rod: power ×2/3, gold-only → power ×1, cooldown ×0.85, catches at least Rare (`minTier`, replaces `goldOnly`;
  gold-only + Golden Hook made it ~4× a Tier IV in coins). Gatling cooldown ×0.4 → ×0.5. Tsunami power 1.5 → 1.25, cooldown
  0.9 → 0.85, radius 1.8 → 1.35, stun +3 → +2. Storm Chain power 1.4 → 1.25, cooldown 0.85 → 0.9, +2 → +1 jump, slow 2 → 1.5 s.
- Trigger Finger rare → gold (fire rate 16 → 20) so Gatling is not a near-certainty.

*Monster curve (skilled builds out-ran HP; monsters died as they surfaced)*
- `rules.ts` `monsterHpCurve`: `55 × 1.165^(t−1)` → `45 × earlyEase(t) × 1.18^(t−1) × 1.36^max(0, t−15)` (gentle until 15,
  then a wall into the tide-20 boss; tide 20 is ~4860 base HP, was ~1000). `earlyEase(t) = min(1, 0.72 + 0.04t)` on HP and
  damage makes tides 1–6 a warm-up.
- Damage `(5 + 1.4t)` → `(4 + 1.15t) × earlyEase × 1.045^max(0, t−10)`.
- Bosses: `bossHpMult (0.55 + 0.045t)` → `1.8 × (curve(t)/curve(10))^0.72` (tide 10 ×1.8, tide 20 ×17.9); boss tides are now
  the run's gates instead of 4-second kills.
- `monsters.ts`: newly unlocked type featured ×3 → ×2; Piranha packs 4–6 → 3–5; Armored Crab HP ×1.7 → ×1.1, damage 24 → 17
  (the tide-7 crab wall was the main casual killer). Brutes from tide 8 (was 7), chance `0.06 + 0.012(t−7)` ≤ 0.25 →
  `0.05 + 0.01(t−8)` ≤ 0.2. Lightning `10 + 0.8t` → `8 + 0.6t` (EndlessMode + mirror).

*Danger (D0–D4 all 100%; D1 was easier than D0 because elites paid ×3 and always dropped a chest)*
- Every level: +7% monster/boss HP and +5% monsters. D5 HP ×1.4 → ×1.1, bosses ×1.6 → ×1.25 (on top of the per-level HP).
- Elites ×2.5 → ×3 HP; reward ×3 coins/xp → ×1.5; chest always → 25% (EndlessMode.onKill, base.ts + mirrors). Elite
  chance `0.05 + 0.01t` ≤ 0.3 → ≤ 0.25. `DANGER_LEVELS` texts updated.

*Captains (all 100%; Willy could never change his only rig; Pip's cursed trinkets stacked without limit)*
- Lucky Pip: cursed trinkets `max: 2` each (`rollOffer` gives a normal offer once all are maxed); Luck +50 → +40.
- One-Line Willy: buying a rig with the slot full now swaps it (old one sold, `buy` returns `swapped`); half his rig offers
  are a twin of his rig at its tier; rig power ×4 → ×8.
- The Harpooner: 4 rig slots (was 6), monster coins ×3 → ×1.25, stats +15 harpoon/+10 fire rate removed. Not fishing means
  it never stops to reel, so it dodges and shoots more: it needs a smaller boat.
- The Net Witch: rigs ×2 (`rigPower: 2`), same 6 slots (she had no answer to the tide-20 pair).
- The Merchant: interest +30 → +15, Max HP −25 → −50. Ingrid speed −15 → −10. Abyss-Touched rot −0.6 → −0.4 HP/s.

*Harbour (max added little before; with a real curve the Dice Cup was the strongest part)*
- Dice Cup 2 levels (15, 40) → 1 level (30); Market Stall 60 → 80 pearls. Full Harbour is now 534 pearls (was 539).

*Shop*
- With every rig slot taken, owned rigs are offered ×5 (was ×2.2) so a full boat still sees merges.

**Bot changes** (only where the old policy was clearly unrealistic): the casual bot still buys the priciest offer, but
weights a weapon rig ×4 while it has no harpoon/charge/drone at all (it used to sail tides 2–6 with only fishing rigs);
the swap rule for one-slot captains is valued as new rig minus old rig (a lone Chum Bucket or Ink Jet counts ×0.2). Added
`--debug` damage-by-source lines (`dmg by {...}` per tide and a `DIED` line).

**Still missed, and why**
- *Average win 5.7% (target 15–25%) and skilled 67% (target 40–55%).* The two bots are about 6 tides apart (median 17 vs
  23), and no single curve puts both in range. Ablations (skilled bot with one policy part swapped for the average's, 150 runs,
  measured mid-tuning when the skilled bot won 53%): its win rate falls from 53% to 25% without combo/evolution hints, to 29% without "sell the weakest rig for a
  better one" and to 39% with one reroll instead of up to five (keeping no interest reserve: 54%, no effect). At an earlier
  curve where it won 95%, the average's whole shop policy took it to 50% and the average's dodging only to 83%. Most of
  the gap is shop skill, not the game's numbers. The curve sits between the two targets;
  skilled players get their 40–55% at Danger 2–3.
- *Combos in >60% of skilled runs* (Iron Keel 83%, Blast Fishing 81%, Deadeye 80%, Overdrive 78%): each is two cheap,
  common items and a skilled run buys about 50 items, so the pairs come together on their own. The bonuses are small.
- *Willy Midas Rod 83%*: his one rig starts as a Bobber, so it is the natural evolution. Willy (23%) is just under the 25%
  floor; he has no weapon unless he swaps.
- *Boss 20 for average 64 s, and only 62% of the average runs that reach tide 20 kill it*: at the top of the target; the
  pair of bosses is the run's last gate.
- *Danger 0 and 1 are close* (67 vs 63%): elites still pay for themselves in part.
